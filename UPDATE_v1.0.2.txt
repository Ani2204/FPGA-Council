  Stage 1: "Requirements incomplete" → NOT APPROVED

  Stage 1: Complete spec created → APPROVED

KEY INSIGHT
-----------

The stages were designed with different philosophies:

  Stage 0: PERMISSIVE - Approve viable ideas, make assumptions
  Stage 1: DOCUMENTARY - Document requirements, always approve
  Stage 3: CRITICAL - Review architecture, can reject
  Stage 7: FINAL - Production readiness, can reject

We've now aligned the prompts with these philosophies.
